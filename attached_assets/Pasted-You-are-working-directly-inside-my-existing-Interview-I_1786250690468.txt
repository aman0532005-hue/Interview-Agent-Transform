# 4. REPLACE FAKE AI BEHAVIOR

This is extremely important.

The interviewer currently feels fake because it tends to appreciate almost every answer.

Fix this completely.

The AI must NOT automatically say:

"Great answer!"
"Excellent!"
"Perfect!"
"That's absolutely correct!"

unless the candidate's actual answer deserves that evaluation.

The AI must be willing to disagree.

For every answer, objectively evaluate:

1. Technical correctness
2. Relevance
3. Completeness
4. Depth
5. Communication quality
6. Misconceptions
7. Missing concepts
8. Overall quality

Classify every answer as exactly one of:

* correct
* partially_correct
* incorrect
* incomplete
* irrelevant
* uncertain

Return/store structured evaluation data.

Example:

A technically wrong answer should produce an evaluation such as:

verdict: incorrect
overallScore: low
mistakes: [...]
feedback: explain the actual misconception
nextAction: clarify

NOT:

"Great answer! Let's move on."

A partially correct answer should identify:

* what was correct
* what was missing
* what was wrong

An excellent answer should:

* acknowledge it briefly
* increase difficulty
* move toward deeper reasoning

"I don't know" should NOT be treated as a correct answer.

An irrelevant answer should be identified as irrelevant.

A one-word answer should be treated according to its actual usefulness.

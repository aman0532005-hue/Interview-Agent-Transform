CURRENT PROBLEM:
The UI looks good, but the interview experience currently behaves too much like a static/scripted chatbot. It does not genuinely evaluate the candidate's answers, score performance, adapt questions based on answers, or generate meaningful final feedback.

THIS IS A HACKATHON FOR PROBLEM STATEMENT 2: "THE INTERVIEW AGENT".

The implementation MUST demonstrate a real multi-turn adaptive technical interview.

CORE FLOW:

1. Candidate starts an interview.

2. Agent asks a technical question based on the candidate's curriculum/profile.

3. Candidate submits an answer.

4. The system evaluates that answer BEFORE asking the next question.

5. Evaluation must consider:
   - technical correctness
   - depth of understanding
   - clarity
   - practical/application understanding
   - misconceptions
   - missing concepts

6. Generate a numerical score from 0-10 for each answer.

7. Generate a short internal evaluation object such as:

{
  "score": 7,
  "technicalCorrectness": 8,
  "depth": 6,
  "clarity": 7,
  "practicalUnderstanding": 6,
  "strengths": ["..."],
  "weaknesses": ["..."],
  "missingConcepts": ["..."],
  "needsFollowUp": true,
  "reason": "..."
}

8. DO NOT expose all internal reasoning/chain-of-thought to the candidate. Only show concise feedback appropriate for a technical interview.

9. Based on the evaluation, dynamically choose the next question.

IMPORTANT:
The next question MUST depend on the previous answer.

Examples:
- Strong answer -> increase difficulty or move toward system design.
- Weak answer -> ask a simpler clarification/follow-up.
- Partially correct answer -> probe the missing concept.
- Candidate mentions something interesting -> ask a deeper question about it.
- Candidate gives an incorrect technical claim -> challenge/correct it through a follow-up question.

10. The interview must cover at least 8 questions across at least 4 different curriculum days/topics, satisfying the hackathon requirement.

11. Questions must NOT simply be a hardcoded list of 8 questions.

12. Maintain interview state throughout the session:
   - questions asked
   - candidate answers
   - evaluations
   - topics covered
   - current difficulty
   - strengths
   - weaknesses
   - total score

13. At the end generate a structured interview report.

FINAL REPORT MUST INCLUDE:

- Overall score /100
- Technical score
- Communication score
- Problem-solving/system-thinking score
- Topic-wise performance
- Strongest areas
- Weakest areas
- Important misconceptions
- Recommended topics to revise
- Interview difficulty reached
- Short hiring-style assessment
- Question-by-question score history

Example:

Overall: 78/100
Level: Intermediate

RAG: 84%
Vector Databases: 72%
Prompt Engineering: 91%
Agentic AI: 65%

Strengths:
- Good understanding of retrieval concepts
- Strong prompt engineering knowledge

Weaknesses:
- Weakness in agent architecture
- Limited understanding of failure handling

Recommendation:
"Candidate demonstrates solid intermediate-level AI engineering knowledge but should strengthen production agent architecture and reliability."
